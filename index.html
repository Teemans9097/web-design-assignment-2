Quality Education For Every Child

It promotes inclusive and equitable quality education and lifelong learning opportunities for all people in Sierra Leone.

WHAT IS QUALITY EDUCATION FOR?

Quality Education focus on providing access to quality learning, improving literacy, supporting teachers, and creating equal learning opportunities for children and youth in Sierra Leone.

Targets of Quality Education

Free Education

Provide free primary and secondary education for all.

Equal Access

Ensure equal educational opportunities for girls and boys.

Teacher Training

Improve teaching quality and learning skills nationwide.

Digital Learning

Support modern technology and online education systems.

Our Initiatives in Sierra Leone

School Support

Providing books, uniforms and classroom materials.

Teacher Programs

Training teachers for better education delivery.

Girl Child Education

Encouraging equal opportunities for girls in schools.
